🏆Welcome to Worlds

The World Championship is the Super Bowl of League of Legends: one month, the best 19 teams on the planet, one trophy. This guide covers everything — no prior knowledge needed.

🆕 Brand new to LoL esports?

Start here. League of Legends is a 5-vs-5 strategy game; the pro season ends with one giant world tournament. Teams qualify through their regional leagues (Korea, China, Europe, the Americas, and more).

How a match works: two teams of five pick from 160+ champions, then battle on one map. Destroy the enemy's base to win the game. Most matches are "best of 3" or "best of 5" — first to more wins takes the series.

⚔️ The format in 20 seconds

  • Play-In (Oct 15–18): 4 underdog teams fight for 1 remaining spot
  • Swiss Stage (Oct 23–31): 16 teams, 5 rounds — win 3 to advance, lose 3 and you're out
  • Knockouts (Nov 3–8): the last 8 teams in a single-elimination bracket — lose once, go home
  • Grand Final (Nov 14): best of 5 at Barclays Center, Brooklyn, for the Summoner's Cup

📜 The story going in

T1 (Korea) are the three-time defending champions — they've won 2023, 2024 and 2025, and their mid laner Faker is the most famous esports player ever. No team has ever won four in a row.

Chasing them: Gen.G and Hanwha Life (Korea's other titans), Bilibili Gaming (twice a finalist, still chasing their first title), and Europe's G2 — the West's best hope.

🗓️
The journey is a three-city tour. It starts in Los Angeles (Play-In), moves to Allen, Texas near Dallas (Swiss + knockouts), and ends at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn — the first East Coast Worlds final ever.

🗓️Schedule

A month-long road trip across America. Daily match times are announced by Riot closer to each stage — those slots stay ⚠︎ TBA until then. All times below are local to the venue.

🇺🇸 Stop 1 — Los Angeles

Riot Games Arena · Play-In stage

Oct 15–18. The LCS studio arena (capacity ~300) hosts four qualifying teams in an intimate, do-or-die bracket.

🤠 Stop 2 — Allen, Texas

Credit Union of Texas Event Center · Swiss + Knockouts

Oct 23 – Nov 8. A Dallas suburb arena (8,100 seats) hosts the main event — 16-team Swiss, then quarters and semis.

🗽 Stop 3 — Brooklyn, NYC

Barclays Center · Grand Final

Nov 14. Nineteen thousand seats for the final — the first time the East Coast hosts a Worlds final.

⭐Players to know

Eight stars who could own this tournament. Positions: Top / Jungle / Mid / Bot (ADC) / Support — the five roles on every team. Full 19-team rosters drop closer to Worlds, so team pages show ⚠︎ TBD until then.

🎓
New to the roles? Top laners duel in isolation up top. Junglers roam the map to ambush and secure objectives. Mids control the center of the action. Bots (ADCs) deal sustained damage late-game. Supports protect and enable everyone else. "Farm" means earning gold by killing minions.
